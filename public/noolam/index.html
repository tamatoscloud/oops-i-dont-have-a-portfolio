    From the loom

    Two weavers, one pallu.

    A Kanchipuram silk is woven by two people sharing one pit loom. The body, border and pallu are woven separately and interlocked by hand — the korvai — so the border never frays away from the body.

    We buy directly from weaver families, pay on the day a saree comes off the loom, and tag every piece with its weave, zari type and the weeks it took.
